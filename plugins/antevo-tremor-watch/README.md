# Tremor Watch

**How many market relationships stopped behaving this week.**

Every Saturday morning the Antevo Tremor Index is written for the week that
ended on Friday. It follows 753 pairs of markets in seven families: equity
regions, US sectors, credit, bonds, commodities, FX and crypto, and volatility.
A pair has stopped behaving when its correlation is two standard deviations
away from its own previous two years. By Antevo.

**Public. No account, no key.** Install it and start asking.

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/tremor/mcp`

## What you can ask

- Are markets under strain this week?
- Which relationships are breaking, and in which families?
- How does this week compare with 2008 or 2020?
- Is gold still moving with equities?

## What it will not do

Every tool is read-only. Nothing here can place a trade, move money or change a
record. It returns derived statistics only (shares, correlations, z-scores and
percentiles), never price or index levels. Every answer names its week and how
to cite it. Descriptive, not a forecast: it measures how many relationships are
breaking at once and says nothing about where prices go next. Not investment
advice.
