# The 07:00 Brief

**Your agent knows the markets by 07:00, every day. We read them so you don't have to.**

Every morning, one sharp read on markets and the world: what moved, what it
means, what to watch today, and the risks building underneath. The Market Map,
Big Picture, the Three Minute Brief, the Risk Radar, and notes on markets, AI,
energy and geopolitics. By Antevo.

**Public. No account, no key.** Install it and start asking.

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/0700/mcp`

## What you can ask

- What do I need to know about markets today?
- Which risks are rising this week, and what would prove them wrong?
- What did the brief say on Monday?
- What is the read on energy and geopolitics this morning?

## Make it automatic

In Claude, add a Scheduled task; in ChatGPT, a Task. Set it for 07:05 every
morning and paste a prompt. Your agent has read the markets before you have.

- *Every day, 07:05:* "Read today's 07:00 Brief and tell me the three things that matter today, and what to watch."
- *With Antevo Wealth connected:* "Read today's 07:00 Brief and tell me the three things that matter today, and what they mean for my holdings."
- *Every Monday, 07:05:* "From The 07:00 Brief's Risk Radar, tell me which risks are rising this week and what to watch."

The brief is out around 07:00 Zurich time. In Asia, pick a time after 14:00
Singapore time.

**Prefer email?** Get the brief in your inbox each morning: [antevo.ch/executive](https://antevo.ch/executive)

## What it will not do

Every tool is read-only. Nothing here can place a trade, move money or change a
record. It returns Antevo's editorial text only, never market price data. Every
answer carries its source and its date. Editorial commentary, not investment
advice.

Full tool list: <https://antevo.ch/mcp>
