# Antevo Mandates

Your firm's client book — who is due a review, what a meeting needs, where
succession planning is incomplete, and each client's goals, documents and email.

**Signs you in over OAuth 2.1.** Your assistant receives a scoped token, never
your password, and reaches your own firm and nothing else.

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/mandates/mcp`

## What you can ask

- Which clients are due a review?
- Prepare a meeting brief for this client.
- Which clients have incomplete succession planning?
- Record that this review is done and schedule the next one.

## What it can change

Mandates is **not read-only**. Within your own firm it can create and update
client records, record a completed review, set goals, manage documents and link
an email to a client. Converting a prospect or deleting a document returns a plan
first and only runs when you confirm. **No tool here can place a trade or move
money.**

You need an Antevo Mandates firm account: <https://antevo.ch/mandate>
