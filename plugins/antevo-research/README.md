# Antevo Research

Put a question to one of Antevo's research desks and get the desk's own written
answer, built from the research and news it reads every day, with every fact
dated and a note of how recent its reading is.

The desks: economy, geopolitics, shipping, real estate, strategy, commodities,
markets, technology, digital assets, health, rates, mortgages, equities,
technical, ETFs, results, the Swiss market and private markets. Your assistant
picks the desk that fits; for a question that spans two, it asks each, or asks
the strategy desk for the joined-up view.

**Signs you in over OAuth 2.1** with any Antevo account. Your assistant receives a
scoped token, never your password.

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/research/mcp`

## What you can ask

- What does the rates desk make of the next SNB decision?
- How exposed is European shipping to the Red Sea this month?
- What did Nestlé's latest results say, and how did they compare with expectations?
- Which ETFs overlap most with the S&P 500's largest holdings?

## What it will not do

It reads and writes an answer; it changes nothing. It is editorial research, not
investment advice: it never tells you to buy, sell or hold, and the technical
desk never gives a price level a market is expected to reach. It cannot see your
holdings; for what something means for your own money, use Antevo Wealth.
Answers can take a minute or two; the same question asked again within a day
comes back at once.
