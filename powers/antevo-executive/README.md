# Antevo Executive Brief

**Markets, macro and world events, with the date on every read.**

Antevo's daily Executive Brief for your agent: the market regime, themes and risks,
the forward calendar of catalysts, the risk radar, the dated archive, the world-events
map and macro-economic history.

**Public. No account, no key.** Remote MCP server over Streamable HTTP:

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/executive/mcp`

## Install in Kiro

Powers → Add Custom Power → import from GitHub:
`https://github.com/ANTEVO-CH/antevo-mcp/tree/main/powers/antevo-executive`

## What you can ask

- What happened in markets today, and what does the Antevo desk make of it?
- What is on the risk radar right now, and what would prove it wrong?
- Which catalysts land in the next two weeks?
- Show me Swiss inflation since 1990.

## What it will not do

Every tool is read-only. Nothing here can place a trade, move money or change a
record. Every answer carries its source and its date. Intelligence, not investment advice.
