# Tremor Watch

**How many market relationships stopped behaving this week.**

Every Saturday morning the Antevo Tremor Index is written for the week that ended on
Friday. It follows 753 pairs of markets in seven families: equity regions, US sectors,
credit, bonds, commodities, FX and crypto, and volatility. A pair has stopped behaving
when its correlation is two standard deviations away from its own previous two years.

**Public. No account, no key.** Remote MCP server over Streamable HTTP:

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/tremor/mcp`

## Install in Kiro

Powers → Add Custom Power → import from GitHub:
`https://github.com/ANTEVO-CH/antevo-mcp/tree/main/powers/antevo-tremor-watch`

## What you can ask

- Are markets under strain this week?
- Which relationships are breaking, and in which families?
- How does this week compare with 2008 or 2020?
- Is gold still moving with equities?

## What it will not do

Every tool is read-only. Nothing here can place a trade, move money or change a record.
It returns derived statistics only, never price or index levels. Every answer names its
week and how to cite it. Descriptive, not a forecast, and not investment advice.
