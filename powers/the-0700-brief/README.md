# The 07:00 Brief

**Your agent knows the markets by 07:00, every day. We read them so you don't have to.**

Every morning, one sharp read on markets and the world: what moved, what it means,
what to watch today, and the risks building underneath. The Market Map, Big Picture,
the Three Minute Brief, the Risk Radar, and notes on markets, AI, energy and geopolitics.
A new brief lands around 07:00 Zurich time; earlier briefs are available by date.

**Public. No account, no key.** Remote MCP server over Streamable HTTP:

`https://api.antevo.ch/mcp/0700/mcp`

## Install in Kiro

Powers → Add Custom Power → import from GitHub:
`https://github.com/ANTEVO-CH/antevo-mcp/tree/main/powers/the-0700-brief`

## What you can ask

- What do I need to know about markets today?
- Which risks are rising this week, and what would prove them wrong?
- What did the brief say on Monday?
- What is the read on energy and geopolitics this morning?

## What it will not do

Every tool is read-only. Nothing here can place a trade, move money or change a record.
It returns Antevo's editorial text only, never market price data. Every answer carries
its source and its date. Editorial commentary, not investment advice.
